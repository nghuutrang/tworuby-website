Chalkbook

A grade book and student manager built for teachers.

Chalkbook helps teachers record grades, take attendance, and track each pupil's progress right on the desk — fully offline. When you choose to, you can share results with parents through a private lookup portal. No ads, and nothing about your pupils leaves your device unless you publish it.

What Chalkbook does

Every feature answers one question: does it save the teacher time in the classroom? Chalkbook is offline-first — it works with no internet — and syncs to the cloud only when you ask it to.

Grade book

Enter grades directly on your device using grading scales for different countries. Organise pupils by class and term, and see the whole gradebook at a glance.

Attendance & daily records

Take attendance and keep daily notes for each pupil. A private note field stays on your device and is never shared with anyone.

Parent lookup portal

When you publish a class, each pupil's parent gets a private link to look up that pupil's grades and attendance on the web — no parent account needed.

Export to Excel, PDF & CSV

Turn any class into a spreadsheet or report in one tap, so you always have your own copy in a standard format.

Backup & restore

Back your data up to your own account and restore it on a new device, so a lost or replaced phone never means lost records.

Works everywhere, 6 languages

Fully offline-first, available in English, Vietnamese, French, German, Japanese and Korean.

Signing in with Google

Chalkbook works without any account. You only sign in with Google (or Apple) when you want cloud features — publishing a class to the parent portal, or backing up and restoring your data.

When you sign in, Chalkbook asks Google only for your basic profile and email address. We use them to:

  • Identify your account so your classes sync to the right place and can be restored on a new device.
  • Show your name and email inside the app so you know which account you are signed in to.

Chalkbook does not read your Gmail, Google Drive, Calendar or contacts, and it never posts anything on your behalf. Your private notes and pupil data stay on your device and are sent to the cloud only when you choose to publish or back up. For full details, see our Privacy Policy and Terms & Conditions.
